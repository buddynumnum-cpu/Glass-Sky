# Glass Sky

A cozy mobile god game prototype. Your phone is a glass-lidded world, and tiny followers live under the glass.

**Play the prototype:** https://buddynumnum-cpu.github.io/Glass-Sky/ (open on a phone, tap "Tap to begin", allow motion access, then tilt).

## Stage 1: the glass box
- Forest clearing seen from above, with tilt parallax
- Treetops and vines pressed against the glass
- Tap to knock; followers look up at you
- Tap hearts stuck to the glass to collect faith
